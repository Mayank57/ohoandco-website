Can you add our company branding?

Yes, on the card and on the box. Send us your logo and we'll share a mockup for your approval before anything is printed.

Is there a minimum order?

Yes, 25 hampers.

What is the last order date?

Custom Branded boxes: 3rd week of October. Ready-to-ship boxes: until about October 30

What about dietary needs?

All food is sealed, vegetarian and labelled for allergens. Tell us if your team has specific needs and we'll plan around them.

How do we pay?

A 50% advance locks your delivery slot, and the balance is due on delivery.

Do you only do Diwali?

Diwali is our first season. Ask us about New Year and work-anniversary boxes.
